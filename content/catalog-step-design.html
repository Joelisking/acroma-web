How should the catalog step work?

The full catalog form has name, price, stock, category, variants, tags, image. Three options for what to show in onboarding:

A

Quick-add list — name + price only

A minimal inline row (name + price), tap "Add another" to keep going. Items stack up in a simple list. Fastest for getting a few things in — zero friction. They flesh out descriptions and images in the catalog later.

Prompt + redirect to catalog
